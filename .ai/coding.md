# Coding Guidelines

## TypeScript

Never use any.

Prefer explicit types.

Use interfaces/types when it improves readability.

---

## Svelte

Use Svelte 5 runes.

Prefer load() over onMount() whenever possible.

Avoid unnecessary reactive state.

Keep components focused.

---

## Components

Extract repeated UI.

Keep components easy to read.

---

## Naming

Descriptive names.

Avoid abbreviations.

Use consistent naming.

---

## Performance

Avoid unnecessary reactivity.

Avoid duplicate fetching.

Avoid large libraries/prefer optimized-smaller alternatives.

Avoid unnecessary hydration/rerenders.

---

## Dependencies

Think carefully before installing packages.

Prefer browser APIs.

---

## Reviews

Whenever writing code, also consider

- accessibility
- bundle size
- readability
- maintainability
